# Landmark artwork — version 3.5

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool. Six sprites per atlas; theme-specific prompts follow. Crops are measured from alpha bounds in LandmarkAssets.java. LCD colors are mapped at load time through LcdPalette. Damage splits the same sprite into fragments; the paper theme adds torn cream edges. No existing character art was replaced.

## Paper
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent game sprite atlas, one square sheet with exactly SIX separate buildings in a strict 3 column by 2 row grid, generous transparent gutters. Each cell has one entire isolated intact building with full spire and base, no overlaps, no labels, no ground or sky. Top row left to right: traditional Japanese shiro castle (white plaster walls, stacked dark teal sweeping tile roofs, stone base); Eiffel Tower (bronze lattice iron, open arch legs); Leaning Tower of Pisa (ivory stacked arcaded circular tiers, visibly leaning right). Bottom row left to right: New York Trump Tower (dark bronze glass skyscraper, recognizable stepped setback silhouette); Burj Khalifa (silver-blue tiered tapering skyscraper, very long fine spire); Taj Mahal (ivory central onion dome, symmetrical facade and four slender minarets). Style: colored-pencil drawing on layered cut paper, cream cut edges, paper puppet theater, charming detailed miniature architecture, crisp silhouettes. Fill each equal square cell independently and preserve natural landmark proportions. Real alpha transparency, no checkerboard, no text, no Godzilla. Intended for individually cropped destructible building game sprites.

Follow-up edit: Remove all background, shadows and glow; preserve the six buildings and cream cut edges, positions, sizes and details; real alpha transparency.
